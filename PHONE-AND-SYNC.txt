Blunder Lab on your iPhone and iPad, with everything synced

You do this once. It takes about ten minutes and needs a free GitHub account.
GitHub does two jobs: it hosts the web app so your phone can open it, and it stores
your Blunder Lab data in a private "gist" that only your account can read.

A. MAKE THE ACCOUNT AND PUT THE APP ONLINE (on your computer)
1. Go to github.com and sign up (free).
2. Top right, press + and "New repository". Name it blunderlab, leave it Public,
   press "Create repository".
3. On the next page click "uploading an existing file".
4. Open the unzipped blunderlab folder, select everything INSIDE it (Ctrl+A) and drag
   it all onto the page. Wait until every file is listed, then press "Commit changes".
5. In the repository open Settings, then Pages. Under "Branch" choose main and
   / (root), press Save.
6. After a minute or two your app is live at
       https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/blunderlab/
   The app's code is public there. Your data is not in it.

B. MAKE THE SYNC TOKEN (on your computer)
1. On github.com click your picture, then Settings, Developer settings,
   Personal access tokens, Tokens (classic).
2. "Generate new token (classic)". Note: Blunder Lab. Expiration: No expiration.
   Tick ONLY the box called gist. Press Generate.
3. Copy the token (it starts with ghp_). You only see it once, so keep a copy
   somewhere safe, such as your password manager.
   The token can only read and write your gists, nothing else on your account.

C. TURN ON SYNC ON EVERY DEVICE
- PC and laptop: open Blunder Lab (the extension), Games tab, scroll to
  "Sync between devices", paste the token, press "Turn on sync".
- iPhone and iPad: open the address from step A6 in Safari, do the same on the Games
  tab. Then press Share and "Add to Home Screen" to get an app icon.
The first device uploads what it has. Every other device downloads it. From then on
each one syncs by itself a few seconds after anything changes, and every few minutes.

WHAT SYNCS
Reviews (with the games), puzzle progress, opening and endgame progress, your
goals and streaks, games against the engine, your repertoire, game rating calibration, your chess.com username and your game history.
The review depth setting stays per device: phones start on Quick.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Offline: once the web app has been opened with a connection, it opens without one.
  Puzzles, the trainer and saved reviews work; new games and syncing wait until you are
  back online. Made-up puzzles stay on the device that made them.
- Let the computer do the reviewing. It is much faster than a phone, it checks for new
  games every minute, and the finished review then appears on your phone.
- If your phone's browser is not allowed to read chess.com directly, Blunder Lab says
  so and carries on with what the computer has synced.
- To update the web app later, upload the new files to the same repository the same
  way (step A4). They replace the old ones.
- Lost the token or want to revoke it? Delete it on GitHub (same page as B1), make a
  new one, and paste the new one on each device.
