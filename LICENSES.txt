Blunder Lab bundles these open-source parts:

- Stockfish chess engine, WebAssembly build "stockfish.js" 19 (lite, single-threaded)
  by Nathan Rugg and the Stockfish developers. GNU General Public License v3.
  Licence text: engine/COPYING-stockfish.txt
  Source: https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js and https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish
- chess.js 1.4.0 by Jeff Hlywa. BSD 2-Clause licence. https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js
- Chess pieces by Colin M.L. Burnett ("cburnett"), licensed GPLv2+ (also GFDL and BSD).
- Opening names from lichess-org/chess-openings. CC0 public-domain dedication.

Blunder Lab's own code (the files in this folder outside engine/, js/chess.js, pieces/ and
data/) is included here in source form and may be used under the GNU GPL v3 as well.

Blunder Lab is not affiliated with Chess.com. It reads your public games through
Chess.com's public data API. Move ratings use Chess.com's published expected-points
bands, but the engine, depth and details differ, so ratings will not always match theirs.
